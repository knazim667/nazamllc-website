What does Nazam LLC do?

Nazam LLC builds practical AI automation systems, reporting dashboards, workflow automations, and marketplace growth systems for small businesses and Amazon/Walmart sellers.

Who do you help?

We help small businesses that need faster lead follow-up, booking workflows, chatbots, CRM automation, dashboards, and internal workflow support. We also help Amazon and Walmart sellers improve listings, reporting, inventory alerts, and seller operations.

Do I need to understand AI to work with you?

No. You bring the business problem. We translate it into a clear workflow, explain the plan in plain language, and build the system around your current tools.

Can you build an AI receptionist or chatbot?

Yes. We can build AI chatbots and receptionist-style systems that answer common questions, collect customer details, route inquiries, and support appointment booking.

Can you automate lead follow-up?

Yes. Lead follow-up automation can capture new inquiries, organize them in a CRM or spreadsheet, send follow-up messages, and alert your team when action is needed.

Can you help Amazon and Walmart sellers?

Yes. We support marketplace sellers with listing reviews, keyword and competitor research, reporting dashboards, inventory/order alerts, and workflow automation.

What happens during the free audit?

We review your website, workflow, or marketplace store, identify one important bottleneck, map the automation opportunity, and share a practical next step.

Because Nazam LLC does not invent performance claims. Project examples show what can be built; future client results should be supported by real evidence.
