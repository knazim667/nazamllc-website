VIII. FAQ / Answers · 08 questions 008 / 009

Common questions.

Everything you need to know before booking a call. If your question isn't here, email admin@nazamllc.com — we respond within one business day.

How quickly can you get started?

Within 48 hours of our discovery call, you'll have a written scope and timeline. Most projects kick off within one week — we don't run multi-month sales cycles.

How is pricing structured?

Fixed-price projects only. You get a written scope with a total cost before we start — no hourly billing, no "it depends" estimates, no surprise invoices at the end of the month.

Do you work with clients outside the US?

Yes. We work fully remote with clients across the US and internationally. The Wyoming LLC structure keeps everything legal, transparent, and above board regardless of where you're located.

What if I'm not satisfied with the result?

Every milestone is tied to a deliverable you can review before we move forward. If something doesn't meet the written scope, we fix it — no disappearing acts, no "that's out of scope" deflection. We document what success looks like in week one and hold ourselves to it.

What tools and platforms do you use?

Automation: n8n, Make, Zapier. Development: FastAPI, React, Python. Marketplace: Amazon Seller Central, Walmart Connect. AI: OpenAI, Anthropic, custom fine-tuned agents. We pick the right tool for the job — not the one we're most comfortable billing for.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes — monthly retainer packages are available for ongoing automation maintenance, marketplace management, and continued product development. You own everything; the retainer just keeps the machine running and improving.

How do AI automations actually save me time?

We start with a workflow audit to identify your highest-value bottlenecks. Then we build automations that run 24/7 in the background — PPC management, lead qualification, client onboarding, inventory monitoring. Most clients reclaim 10–20 hours per week within the first month.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes — scroll up to the Labs section for five documented real-world outcomes across automation, AI agents, and finance. For a full portfolio walkthrough, email admin@nazamllc.com and we'll schedule a screen-share.
