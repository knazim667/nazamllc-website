II. Who this is for · Retail investors, not gamblers 002 / 007
What's broken

Most Financial Content Is Designed to Keep You Confused.

Financial media profits from your anxiety. Cable news needs you to tune in every day. Reddit memes pump stocks into oblivion. Video gurus sell courses about strategies they don't actually trade. The result is retail investors making decisions on emotion, noise, and FOMO.

The investors who consistently win aren't smarter. They're more disciplined. They understand market structure. They read charts as maps, not magic. They know when money flows between sectors and why. And increasingly, they're using AI tools to process more data faster than any human analyst can. That's what we teach here — and we don't charge you for the fundamentals.

III. What we cover · 04 subject areas 003 / 007
The curriculum

Free, and Always Will Be.

Four subject areas, taught the way an operator would want them taught: mechanism first, indicators second.

01 Charts

Technical Analysis

Candlestick patterns and what they actually predict. Volume confirmation and divergence. Moving averages as dynamic support and resistance. RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands without overloading your charts. Chart patterns: flags, wedges, head and shoulders, cup and handle.

02 Rotation

Sector Analysis

Reading sector ETF relative strength charts. How economic data — CPI, PMI, jobs reports — moves sectors. The defensive versus cyclical rotation playbook. Deep dives into energy, tech, healthcare, and financials.

03 AI

AI Tools for Research

AI chatbots for earnings call transcript analysis. What AI-powered stock screeners do and don't do. Using AI to summarize SEC filings and find red flags. Quantitative approaches a retail investor can actually run.

04 Macro

Market Structure & Macro

The Federal Reserve and interest rate policy, simply explained. Yield curve dynamics and what inversions have historically signaled. How institutional positioning — COT reports, dark pool data — provides context. Macro regime identification: inflation, deflation, stagflation.
