Privacy Policy

How Nazam LLC collects, uses, and protects information from visitors to this website and from clients of our services.

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Nazam LLC ("Nazam LLC," "we," "us," or "our") is a Wyoming, USA technology consultancy. This policy explains what information nazamllc.com collects, why, and how you can control it. We keep this deliberately simple: this is a lean, static marketing website, not a platform that stores user accounts.

1. Information We Collect 2. How We Use It 3. Cookies & Third Parties 4. Sharing & Disclosure 5. Data Retention 6. Your Rights 7. Security 8. Children's Privacy 9. Changes to This Policy 10. Contact

01 Information We Collect

This website does not run a contact form, live chat widget, or account system. We only receive information you actively choose to send us, plus standard technical data that any website host collects automatically:

  • Information you send us directly — if you email admin@nazamllc.com or message us via LinkedIn, we receive whatever you include: your name, email address, company details, and the content of your message.
  • Standard server logs — our hosting provider (Hostinger) automatically logs technical data common to all web servers, such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and request timestamps, for security and performance purposes.
  • Font delivery — page fonts are served by Google Fonts, which may log the IP address and user-agent of the request as part of delivering the font files.

02 How We Use It

We use the information above only to:

  • Respond to your inquiry, book a strategy call, or scope a project.
  • Deliver, maintain, and secure the services you engage us for.
  • Keep this website running reliably and diagnose technical issues.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

03 Cookies & Third Parties

This site does not set its own tracking or advertising cookies. Two third-party services are used to deliver the page itself:

  • Google Fonts — loads web font files used across the site. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Hostinger — our hosting provider, which may log standard access data as described above. See Hostinger's Privacy Policy.

If we ever add analytics, chat, or ad tracking in the future, we will update this policy first.

04 Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate (e.g., our email provider or hosting company), bound to use data solely to provide that service.
  • Authorities, if required by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Nazam LLC or others.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets — with notice to affected clients where practical.

05 Data Retention

We retain inquiry and client correspondence for as long as needed to deliver services, satisfy legal or accounting obligations, and maintain a record of engagements — typically no longer than seven years after an engagement ends, unless you request earlier deletion.

06 Your Rights

Regardless of where you are located, you may ask us at any time to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete information we no longer need to retain for legal or contractual reasons.
  • Stop using your information for a particular purpose.

To exercise any of these, email admin@nazamllc.com. We respond within one business day.

07 Security

We use industry-standard measures — HTTPS encryption in transit, restricted access to hosting and email accounts, and credential hygiene — to protect information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable, proportionate steps for a business of our size.

08 Children's Privacy

This website and our services are directed at businesses and individuals over the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and we ask that individuals under 18 not submit information to us.

09 Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services or applicable law evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

10 Contact

Questions about this policy or your information? Reach Nazam LLC, a Wyoming LLC, at admin@nazamllc.com.
