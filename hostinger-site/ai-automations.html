FAQ / Answers · 03 questions 006 / 007
Questions

Before you book.

How do you decide what to automate first?

We map your current workflows, rank the opportunities by ROI, and write a technical specification before anyone writes code. Some businesses need a complex AI agent. Others need a simple three-step workflow. We tell you the truth about which one you're looking at.

What is the difference between a workflow and an AI agent?

A workflow executes rules you define: when this happens, do that. An AI agent makes decisions — it can research, classify, draft, respond, and take action. Most engagements use both, and we only reach for an agent where a rule-based workflow genuinely cannot do the job.

Do I own the automations you build?

Yes. We deploy, document, and hand over. You own everything — code, infrastructure, credentials. No lock-in.
